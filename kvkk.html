Kumbu: Envelope Budget Planner · Updated November 6, 2026 · Berk Sancak

This notice is given under Article 10 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller

Berk Sancak. Contact: berk.kurumsal@hotmail.com.

Data processed

In the app: income, payments, envelopes, entries and savings stay on your phone and are not processed by us.

Speech: when on-device recognition is not available for your language, what you say is processed by Apple Inc. (USA) to turn it into text.

Purchases: purchase information and a random app user ID, processed by Apple and RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to provide Pro.

Usage analytics: anonymous usage events (for example that an entry was made by voice or a setup step was finished), app version, device model, country and a random app ID, processed by Google LLC (USA) through Google Analytics for Firebase to improve the app.

Support: your email address and message if you write to us.

Website: GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts this site and may log IP addresses for security.

Purposes and legal grounds

Providing the features and purchases you use and answering your requests (KVKK Art. 5/2-c, performance of a contract), and running the website securely and measuring how the app is used to improve it (Art. 5/2-f, legitimate interest).

Transfers abroad

Apple, RevenueCat, Google and GitHub are in the USA. Transfers are made with the safeguards in Article 9 of KVKK.

Retention

Support conversations are kept for up to two years. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies. Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months.

Your rights (Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, correction or deletion, to learn who it was transferred to, to object to results against you and to claim compensation. Write to berk.kurumsal@hotmail.com; we reply within 30 days.
