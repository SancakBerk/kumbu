What it does

Talk or type

Tap the mic and say "groceries 42" or "taxi 18 last night". No mic? Type the same sentence.

Several at once

"Groceries 62 and gas 40" becomes two entries. If one is unclear, Kumbu asks about that one only.

Payday to payday

See what you can spend per day until the next paycheck, and how many days are left.

Fixed payments

Add rent, bills, subscriptions and installments once. If a bill comes in higher, say so and Kumbu evens it out.

Savings jars

Give each goal a jar and add to it by voice: "put 50 in vacation". When the month ends, move what's left into one.

No account, no bank login

Your entries stay on your iPhone. Kumbu never asks for your bank details.

Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Entries are kept on your phone. Speech is turned into text on the iPhone when your language allows it; otherwise Apple's servers do it. Gold and currency prices need a connection.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese and Japanese. The app follows your iPhone's language.

What happens after the free trial?

The yearly price is charged on the date shown before you start, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Cancel in iPhone Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Your entries are part of your iPhone backup. Pro can also export everything as a CSV file.
